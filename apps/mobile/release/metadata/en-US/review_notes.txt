Third Roll uses original branding and house rules. No licensed affiliation is claimed. There is no account or sign-in, no remote multiplayer, no purchases and no required game-server connection. No demo credentials are needed.

1. Launch, enter a display name, choose five, six, eight or ten dice and zero to three computer opponents. Start Game performs the first roll automatically.
2. Tap dice to hold them, reroll the others up to twice and choose an unused scoring row. Full Straight is 50 points; Castle needs distinct triples; All of a Kind requires the entire hand to match.
3. Pause and resume a saved game. Scores and history are local, separated by dice mode. They are not online rankings.
4. Open Help for rules, build/version and optional diagnostic preview. Cancelling sends nothing; explicit sharing opens the OS sheet with the previewed allowlist text.
5. Test deletion only with disposable data and explicit confirmation. Save recovery preserves damaged data until an explicit choice.
