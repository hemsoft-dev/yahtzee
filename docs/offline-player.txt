THIRD ROLL OFFLINE

For Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Extract the ZIP, then double-click the application. Nothing needs to be installed.
You do not need internet, Convex, Node.js, or an account.

Enter your name, choose a dice count and up to three AI opponents, and play.
The first roll is automatic. Click dice to hold them, reroll, then choose a score.

Completed scores are saved on this PC under your Windows user profile, in
%APPDATA%\Yahtzee Offline. Copy that entire folder while the app is closed if
you want to back it up. Moving the executable does not move or erase your scores.
The folder keeps the game's earlier name, so existing scores carry over.
The top ten scores for each dice count are kept, along with your last 500 games.
These scores are local only and are not shared with an online leaderboard.

Keep the window open until you finish a game. Closing it or choosing Quit Game
abandons the unfinished game. Completed scores remain saved.

This private build is unsigned, so Windows may display a publisher warning.
Only run a copy you trust. It does not need administrator permission.
